# BUG-261123.6: More BaRs weld slower in the logistics test; a busy base halves throughput

## Status: Open
## Severity: Medium
## Version: v2.5.7
## Found In: REVIEW-261123.1 (profiler-driven performance review, 2026-11-23) — to investigate (weld budgets, per-grid limit, stagger, source rescans)

## Description
S103 (10 BaRs, 40 ship blocks) 12.8 welds/s; S104 (20 BaRs, 80 blocks) 4.5 welds/s; S108 (20 BaRs, busy base:
item traffic, docking, sorter) 2.6 welds/s. Plates are conserved in all, so it is throughput, not correctness. Runs were
at WorkSpeed 10 (S103 / S107 finished in ~3 s); rerun at WorkSpeed 3 (261123.24) for comparable numbers.

## Measured at WorkSpeed 3 (261123.24-261123.32)
| BaRs | Ship blocks | Welds/s quiet | Welds/s busy | ServerDoWeld / ServerTryWelding calls |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 40 | 2.15 (S103) | 1.72 (S107) | 47 / 329 |
| 20 | 80 | 1.64 (S104) | 2.13 (S108) | 128 / 1,723 |
| 50 | 200 | 1.09 (S105, 197/200 in 180 s) | — | 648 / 16,880 (4 % of tries weld) |

Plates conserved everywhere (S105: 3,940 used = the 197 welded blocks' need); mod tick avg 0.013 ms, sim >= 0.98.
So not CPU: BaRs wait. The earlier "busy base halves throughput" came from the too-short WorkSpeed-10 runs and does not
hold at WorkSpeed 3. Hypothesis: plates spread thin (S105: ~16 per container over 302 containers, ~20 per block) ->
pulls collect too little, failed blocks are parked by the 15 s failure cooldown for the whole supply group, and more
BaRs means more contention on the same nearly empty containers. Next: `/nanobars test run S105 detail` — the
ServerTryWelding detail lines count skippedByFailCooldown / componentFailures / skippedByAssign / skippedByGridLimit.

## Fix (proposed)
Compare per-method cost and idle-cycle reasons between S103/S104 and S104/S108 (needs the idle-reason counter of
FEAT-261123.4): weld ms budget, MaxSystemsPerTargetGrid, ships reachable per BaR, source rescans after docking /
sorter changes (connection cache invalidation).
