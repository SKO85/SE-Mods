# BUG-260612.12: State.InventoryFull has two conflicting writers — transport drain overwrites the hysteresis latch

## Status: Open
## Severity: Medium
## Version: v2.5.6
## Found In: REVIEW-260612.1 #M5 — `NanobotSystem.Inventory.cs:228`, `NanobotSystem.Operations.cs:400-449`, `NanobotSystem.Grinding.cs:41`

## Description
`CheckAndUpdateInventoryFull` maintains the latch with hysteresis (set ≥100%, clear <90%). But `ServerEmptyTransportInventory` unconditionally writes `State.InventoryFull = !empty` based on the TRANSPORT inventory. During active welding with a 100%-full welder: the latch is set, then the weld tick's `ServerEmptyTransportInventory(false)` (with empty transport) clears it — so in WeldBeforeGrind, `MultiGrind`'s `if (State.InventoryFull) return;` gate sees false and one grind op runs against a full welder (output parked in transport; not lost, but the gate and hysteresis are defeated), and the per-tick flag flips churn state sync. Collect re-checks via `CheckAndUpdateInventoryFull`; grind does not.

## Root Cause
Two semantically different "full" signals (welder-block latch vs transport state) share one field.

## Fix
Stop writing `State.InventoryFull` from `ServerEmptyTransportInventory` (let `CheckAndUpdateInventoryFull` own the latch), or make the write respect the hysteresis (only set, never clear, from the transport path).
