# BUG-261123.2: A BaR stuck at ~99% inventory does not show "Inventory full"

## Status: Done
## Severity: Low
## Version: v2.5.7 (all versions)
## Found In: harness S89 (push targets full), 2026-11-23

## Description
The inventory-full latch was set only at 100% of the welder volume. Ground ore rarely fills the last bit exactly, so
the BaR stopped at ~99% (S89: 15.47 of 15.625 m³, 873 kg left in the transport): it could not grind or collect, but
the terminal showed "needs grinding" instead of "Inventory full".

## Fix
`NextInventoryFull`: set from 98%, clear below 90% (unchanged hysteresis); also used for the world-load seed.
Grinding and collecting stop at 98% instead of 100%; welding is not gated by the flag.

## Scenario
SCN-INV-002 — unit:InventoryFullLatchTests; ingame:S89 (all storage full: "Inventory full" reported, resumes after
space is freed, no ore lost).

## Resolution
`NanobotSystem.Operations.cs` (`NextInventoryFull`), `NanobotSystem.Init.cs` (load seed). BuildId 261123.15.
Verified in game, BuildId 261123.15: S89 "Inventory full" reported, all 124155 kg iron conserved (rig 82221, BaR 41062,
transport 873), derelicts ground 15 s after space was freed; S04 / S10 green.
