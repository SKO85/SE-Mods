# BUG-261122.14: Large BaR clusters work 35-45 % slower than small clusters with the same BaR count

## Status: Fixed
## Severity: Medium
## Version: v2.5.6
## Found In: harness S64-S69 (2026-11-22, BuildId 261122.16)

## Description
Same 20 BaRs, same targets, all BaRs busy (barsWorkedPct 1.0): 2 clusters of 10 vs 10 clusters of 2.
Weld 35.9 vs 55.7 blocks/s (S64/S65), grind 20.4 vs 37.5 (S66/S67), mixed 13.6 vs 21.0 (S68/S69).
weldBudgetPeak / grindBudgetPeak 2; ServerDoWeld max 9-15 ms (one engine weld spike nearly fills the 8 ms budget).

## Hypotheses
- One scanner per cluster: a 10-BaR cluster shares one capped, sorted candidate list (fair-share truncation),
  so members pile on the same nearest blocks / grids and wait on claims; small clusters each sort from their own
  position and spread out.
- Claims: members of one cluster skip each other's claimed blocks more often (lock-on churn).
## Next
Rerun S64/S65 with profiler detail (claim skips, TruncateGridAware, per-member target overlap); add
RebuildClusters / SortAndCapGridCandidates to the S64 perf list.

## Scenario
SCN-BUDGET-008 (S64-S69).

## Root Cause (confirmed, harness S64-S67 x2, BuildId 261122.26)
Not distance (avg target distance 53 m large vs 57 m small) and not contention (0 samples of two BaRs on one block;
busy 86-93 % in large clusters). Large-cluster BaRs simply fire less often: weld calls per BaR per second 1.8 (2 x 10)
vs 5.2 (10 x 2); grind 1550 calls in 40 s vs 21 s. Per-tick weld / grind budgets peaked at 1-2 (not binding).
`NanobotSystem.Update.cs` stagger: with 20 enabled BaRs the mod-wide stagger is 3; a cluster of >= 6 members uses
`min(3, clusterSize - 3)` = 3 groups (each BaR every 3rd cycle), a cluster of 2-5 collapses to 1 group (every cycle).
So the most common farm layout (many BaRs side by side = one big cluster) runs at a third of the speed. The
MaxActionsPerCycle = 1 comment ("throughput is paced by the per-tick budgets") does not hold under this stagger.

## Fix options
- (a) Clusters of any size use 1 group, like 2-5; keep the sim-speed penalty (server under load) and the idle cadence
  stretch; per-tick weld / grind count + ms budgets still cap the heavy work. Cost measured: mod tick avg 0.060 ->
  0.075 ms at 20 BaRs for +55-90 % throughput. Worst case = what small-cluster farms already do today.
- (b) Keep the stagger, compensate with more actions per fire — conflicts with the MaxSystemsPerTargetGrid
  accounting (see MaxActionsPerCycle comment); not recommended.
- (c) Milder: groups = min(modWide, ceil(clusterSize / 10)).

## Resolution (owner chose option a)
`NanobotSystem.ResolveEffectiveGroups`: clusters of any size use 1 stagger group; solo BaRs keep the BUG-102 cap of 2;
the sim-speed penalty (sim < 0.9) and the idle cadence stretch are unchanged; per-tick weld / grind count + ms budgets
cap the heavy work. Unit: StaggerGroupsTests. BuildId 261122.27. Expected: S64/S66 (2 x 10) match S65/S67 (10 x 2).
Verified in game, BuildId 261122.27: S64 (2 x 10) 59.8 welds/s vs S65 (10 x 2) 62.1 (was 35.9 vs 55.7); S66 38.4 ground/s
vs S67 36.7 (was 19.2 vs 35.9). Mod tick avg S64 0.080 ms, S66 0.043 ms, sim 1.00.
