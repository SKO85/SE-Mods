# BUG-261122.10: New damage on a fully repaired grid waits up to EmptyGridRescanDelaySeconds before repair

## Status: Fixed
## Severity: Medium
## Version: v2.5.6 (behaviour since FEAT-017, v2.5.0)
## Found In: harness S57 (2026-11-22, 01_211_018, BuildId 261122.12) — `NanobotSystem.Scanning.Block.cs` empty-grid fast path (`_EmptyGridCache`, ~line 569)

## Description
Once a scan finds nothing to do on a grid, the grid is cached as empty and its blocks are not checked again for
`EmptyGridRescanDelaySeconds` (default 20 s). Damage taken in that window (or a block frame placed by hand) is
not seen until the entry expires. S57: 5 repaired blocks hit again 3 s after the repair were picked up after
~18 s (repair done 19.2 s after the hit); hit again after 30 s idle → ~11 s (12.2 s). Expected: a few seconds
(the normal scan interval). In combat a fully repaired ship gets no repair for up to 20 s after the first hits.

## Root Cause
FEAT-017 trades latency for scan cost; nothing invalidates the empty-grid entry when the grid changes. The mod
has no grid change hooks (no OnBlockAdded / OnBlockIntegrityChanged); `DamageHandler.OnAfterDamage` (server)
only tracks friendly grinding.

## Fix (proposed)
- `DamageHandler.OnAfterDamage`: for any damage with Amount > 0 on an `IMySlimBlock`, stamp
  `Mod.GridChangedAt[grid.EntityId] = ElapsedPlayTime` (ConcurrentDictionary; skip the write if the stamp is < 1 s old).
- Empty-grid fast path: skip only while `emptyTime > changedAt` for that grid.
- Periodic cleanup of stamps older than the delay (existing Mod maintenance).
Cost: one dictionary lookup per damage event; no extra scanning unless a grid actually changed.

## Scenario
SCN-CORE-001 — `unit:GridChangeHandlerTests`; `ingame:S57` detect limit tightened to TargetsUpdateInterval + 3 s (8 s).

## Resolution
- `Handlers/GridChangeHandler.cs` (new): per-grid damage stamp, written at most once a second per grid; empty
  marks taken less than 2 s after a stamp are not trusted (damage is applied on the grid's next update).
  Cleanup every 10 s of stamps older than the delay + 2 s; cleared on unload. No stamps when the skip is off.
- `DamageHandler.OnAfterDamage`: any damage > 0 on a slim block stamps its grid.
- `GridScanCache.InvalidateIfEmpty`: the stamp drops an empty cached cluster result (non-empty ones are kept,
  so grinding does not cost cache hits).
- `AsyncAddBlocksOfGrid`: empty-grid skip only while the grid is unchanged; profiler `rescanAfterDamage`.
- `UpdateSourcesAndTargetsTimer`: an idle-backed-off coordinator that sees damage on a grid it found empty
  goes back to the normal interval (FEAT-071 10 s -> 5 s); `HasDamageOnEmptyGrid` profiled.
- Before (S57, BuildId 261122.13): detected 18.4 s / 11.8 s after the hit.
- Hand-placed block frames (no damage event) are not covered — BUG-261122.11.
