# FEAT-261123.2: Keep per-grid scan results until the grid changes

## Status: Rejected
## Priority: Medium
## Version: v2.5.7+

## Summary
Keep per-grid scan results until the grid changes.

## Motivation
`GridScanCache` expires after 3 s and solo BaRs cannot use it, so a 10,000-block grid is rescanned in full
(11.5 ms) every scan even when nothing changed.

## Design
Keep a grid's candidate list until GridChangeHandler reports damage / added blocks, with a ~30 s safety limit.
Behaviour change: repaint, ownership and projector changes do not report a change and stay stale up to the limit —
owner decision needed.

## Testing
SCN-CLUSTER-004 (unit:CacheTests) + ingame:S99-S102, S57, S72, S23 / S24 (colour changes).
## Resolution
Rejected by the owner (2026-11-23): background-only gain (no frame time), while repaint / ownership / projector
changes would go unnoticed for up to 30 s (visible to players, e.g. grind colour seemingly ignored).
