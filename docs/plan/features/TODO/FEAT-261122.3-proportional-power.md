# FEAT-261122.3: Weld / grind speed follows the power the BaR actually receives

## Status: Proposed (owner 2026-11-22: after v2.5.6)
## Priority: Medium
## Version: v2.5.7+
## Summary
An under-powered BaR works at the share of its welding / grinding power it actually gets (50 % power -> half speed);
it stops only with no power. Makes the admin power settings meaningful (today they are mostly ignored, BUG-261122.9).

## Motivation
`HasRequiredElectricPower` checks the current state's need against grid capacity, so the BaR flips between standby
and welding demand and keeps working at roughly half to full speed whatever the supply. Admins who raise
`MaximumRequiredElectricPower*` expect weak grids to slow the BaR down.

## Design (sketch)
- Request the working demand on the BaR's resource sink while it has work (no flip to standby between actions).
- Supplied ratio from the sink (`SuppliedRatioByType(Electricity)`) scales the weld amount (`IncreaseMountLevel`) and
  the grind amount (`DecreaseMountLevel` / damage); below a small floor (e.g. 5 %) no work and "not enough power".
- Many BaRs on one grid then share the real supply through the game's distributor (today each checks total capacity).
- Release note: weak grids get slower BaRs (gameplay change).

## Testing
Harness S20 becomes a speed check (welding power 2x supply -> ~half speed vs S19); S19 unchanged; new: 4 BaRs on one
battery share the supply. SCN-CORE-003. Unit: amount scaling by supplied ratio.
