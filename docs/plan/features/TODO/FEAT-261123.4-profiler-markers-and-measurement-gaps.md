# FEAT-261123.4: Profiler markers and measurement gaps

## Status: Proposed (REVIEW-261123.1)
## Priority: Medium
## Version: v2.5.7+

## Summary
Profiler markers and measurement gaps.

## Motivation
Several paths have no markers, and the harness cannot show network cost or why BaR cycles do nothing.

## Design
- Markers: grind post-lock checks, `RemoveGrindTargetEverywhere`, power check, reputation path,
  `FriendlyRelationsHandler.Rebuild` (main thread every 15 s), damage / grid-change handlers, per-block scan split.
- Per-frame mod total (drives sim speed); idle-cycle reason counter (S70: 52 % of BaR cycles did nothing).
- Warm-up counted per method (scan methods get steady data); detail timestamps below the second; flush without
  holding the lock (detail runs inflate parents).
- A dedicated-server run of S64 / S70 for state-message and engine network cost.

## Testing
SCN-BUDGET-008 — harness summaries carry the new markers.
